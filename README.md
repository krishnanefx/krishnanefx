### About

I care about two things: **AI deployed for public good**, and **the ethics of how we deploy it**.

That sounds abstract until you look at what it actually requires — building tools that reach the people who need them, designing systems that don't quietly fail the most vulnerable users, and being honest about what AI can and can't responsibly do. That's the work I'm trying to learn how to do well.

Headed to **GovTech Singapore** to work on AI governance and digital public services. Before that, **Stanford Summer Session 2026** — international relations, political science, management science, oral comms. Studying the soft stack as seriously as the technical one.

### Selected work

> Most of what I build falls into one of three buckets: **civic tech**, **information legibility**, or **AI tooling for individual leverage**.

#### 🤝 Civic tech
**Project OneMigrant** — Web lead. A youth-led platform reframed as a community partner directory and noticeboard for migrant workers in Singapore. Built with The Salvation Army's Sojourn Care Centre.

#### 🔍 Information legibility
[**ClearFrame**](https://github.com/krishnanefx/ClearFrame) — Multi-source news aggregation with bias analysis across the political spectrum. Surfaces *how* a story is framed, not just what's reported.

[**News-Scraper**](https://github.com/krishnanefx/News-Scraper) — Pulls global news into structured Obsidian notes, linking articles via AI entity extraction. A second brain that builds itself.

[**Scholar_Scraper**](https://github.com/krishnanefx/Scholar_Scraper) — Collects and analyses academic profiles from major AI/ML conferences. Python + ML pipelines for understanding who's working on what, where.

#### ⚡ AI tooling
[**PriceSniper**](https://github.com/krishnanefx/PriceSniper) — London AI Hack 2025. An AI bid co-pilot for UK contractors that turns a job description into a priced, ready-to-send proposal.

**FilmLab** — Browser-based photo editor inspired by Dehancer. React + WebGL 2.0, 12-stage shader pipeline, 15 film presets.
